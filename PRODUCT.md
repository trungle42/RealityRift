# Storybound

## Register

product

## Users

Players exploring a fairy-tale adventure in a desktop browser, alone with a following companion or with a second keyboard player.

## Product Purpose

Let players inhabit and influence a fairy tale through direct movement, discovery, conversation, challenges, and combat. The user's references are Zelda and Genshin Impact: a traversable world, an embodied hero, and responsive actions.

## Brand Personality

Wonder, warmth, adventure. Original woodland characters, old magic, a stolen spring, and a kingdom shaped by player choices.

## Anti-references

A flat slideshow; a succession of text screens; a world that looks interactive but cannot be explored. Avoid interfaces that cover the scene with decorative panels.

## Design Principles

- The player acts in the world. Story objectives are places to reach and events to influence.
- The world leads; the interface supports orientation and immediate action.
- Make movement, combat, interaction range, danger, and rewards legible through feedback.
- Preserve the existing fairy-tale campaign and StoryProvider integration.
- Keep the adventure playable with the scripted offline storyteller.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Implementation defaults: readable contrast, keyboard navigation, named controls, visible focus, reduced interface motion, and responsive layouts. These defaults do not assert a formal accessibility certification of the 3D game.
